# Ferris wheel passenger ride

Walk or drive to the purple boarding ring beside the wheel (marked on the map).
Stop, then press E / Enter-exit to board. Boarding exits the cart if occupied and
seats the avatar in the lowest gondola. Remote boarding is rejected. The wheel continues its 120-second rotation.
The camera follows the cabin; walking, vehicle input and ride-dispatch updates
pause during the attraction ride. The cabin glass is translucent.

Enter-exit, E, or Back to avatar ends the ride and restores the saved ground
position and normal controls. Switching to train or wheel viewing also exits first.
Exiting from the air is an instant game teleport, not a physical unloading sequence.
