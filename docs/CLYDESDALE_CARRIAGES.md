# Clydesdale carriage loop

Four red Anheuser-Busch-lettered carriages circulate in both web and offline Android builds. Each has a paired Clydesdale hitch with bay coats, white facial blazes and feathered legs, black harnesses, a driver, and a walking gait tied to movement.

The mapped one-way Broadway segment travels south. To include it legally, the route is:

Broadway at Market → south to Walnut → west to 8th → north to Market → west to 14th → south to Clark → east to 8th → north to Market → east to Broadway.

The short Walnut connector accommodates Broadway's one-way direction. All legs follow actual mapped street edges. Four carriages start one quarter of the 816.79-world-unit loop apart; stoplights and traffic can change their spacing during play. They move at 1.65 world units per second and share car traffic's intersection reservations, red lights, queue spacing and collision checks. They remain on this loop, including after a wait, instead of taking ambient traffic's random offscreen relocation.

These are decorative traffic carriages, not a new boarding activity. Their branding is locally rendered lettering, not an official logo asset.
