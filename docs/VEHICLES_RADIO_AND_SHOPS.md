# Vehicles, startup choices and station shops

On each game load, choose one of 20 adult avatar presets and 15 cart configurations. The chooser remembers the previous selection on this browser/device. Avatar presets change clothing, skin, hair and hairstyle; carts change paint, seating capacity, speed, acceleration, brakes, tank size and luggage-rack appearance. Vehicle properties affect gameplay, including passenger capacity. Only the chosen cart and avatar are created.

Busy mode increases the full population to 650 road vehicles and 160 pedestrians (light mode: 120/50). Calmer mode uses 65% of those counts. Distance culling and shared traffic meshes remain enabled.

Stopped sedans, pickups, delivery trucks, motorcycles and bicycles can be entered using E / Enter-exit. Stop before exiting; complete or cancel an active fare before changing vehicles. Wrecked vehicles remain unavailable. Motorcycles are fastest; trucks accelerate more slowly; bicycles use pedal power. The NPC rider disappears when taking a two-wheeler. Passenger fares require at least one passenger seat. Train, helicopter and Ferris wheel continue to use their ride systems.

The World panel contains Vehicle radio while driving: Soulard Blues, River Jazz, Broadway Electronic, Sunset Cruise and Off. These are original synthesized in-game music channels, not broadcasts or licensed commercial recordings. Station selection follows each vehicle. The existing Music toggle mutes music; radio stops on exit and while the page is hidden. Audio begins after a browser user gesture.

Ten named stations inside the playable map receive Fuel & Market facades, pump geometry and signs. The Fuel, food & money panel activates beside a pump while stopped. Gas is $2/L, sold in batches up to 5 L; a partial fill rounds the final charge up to a whole game dollar. Fuel is retained per vehicle, consumed with travel, and an empty tank disables acceleration. Bicycles need no gas. Spawn-cart recovery does not refill the existing cart's tank.

A sandwich costs $8 and restores 40 food / 15 health; water costs $3 and restores 45 water / 5 health, capped at 100. Food and water slowly decrease but do not cause starvation damage in this version. Purchases debit the existing fare wallet and never allow an overdraft. Seventeen glowing cash bundles near stops and stations award $5–$25 once per bundle per game session. Wallet, world, fuel and pickup state remain session-based; only startup choices are saved. Existing passenger fares remain the repeatable paid job. Suggested next jobs: package delivery, station restocking and roadside assistance; these are not yet implemented.

Verification artifacts contain full/light startup, selections, all five vehicle classes, varied speed, empty-fuel behavior, purchase prices and rejection cases, one-time cash pickup, radio selection state, and helicopter/Ferris-wheel regressions. Physical phone performance and audible output require device testing.
