# Daytime, population, Landing landmarks, and the train

## How to try it

- Open **World** and check **Daytime forever** beside the Time of day slider. It holds the sun at noon and disables the slider. Uncheck it to resume the day/night clock. Weather remains independently selectable.
- The default Busy city now has **460 pedestrians** in full graphics and **350** in light graphics: 300 more than each previous setting. Calmer city keeps its existing 65% population option. Distant people still use shared meshes and reduced update frequency.
- Use **Landmarks → Visit / City view** to find Horseshoe Casino, Four Seasons Hotel, HoteLumière, and The Old Spaghetti Factory at Raeder Place. Nearby low-rise Landing buildings receive brick, arched-window, cornice, and storefront textures on their existing road-cleared footprints.
- Choose **World → Train boarding (F7)** or **Landmarks → Union Station · Train ride → Visit**. Walk or stop a vehicle inside the blue boarding circle and press **E / Enter-exit**. The vehicle stays outside. If the train is away, a countdown shows the next arrival; press E again to cancel.
- On board, look around using the normal camera controls. Press E to return safely to the Union Station boarding area. Cart spawning and other rides are disabled during the trip.

## Route and art scope

The original OSM Union Station approach remains in the route. An original closed underground game alignment connects it into a continuous loop, with rail beds, steel rails, tunnel walls, ceilings, and guide lights. The train stops for ten seconds at Union Station on each lap. This is a fictional scenic game route, not an accurate MetroLink service map or schedule.

The new building textures are original procedural facade artwork, not downloaded photographs. The Old Spaghetti Factory location uses OSM node 12181389815, inside Raeder Place building 241172882. Its address was cross-checked with the restaurant's official location page: https://www.osf.com/location/st-louis-mo/ . Other named buildings use the IDs already bundled in the map; map attribution remains OpenStreetMap / ODbL.
